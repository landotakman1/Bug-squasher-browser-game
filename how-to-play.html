How to Play

Objective

Bugs have crashed the production server! Your goal as the new Jr. Developer is to "squash" as many bugs as possible to restore the system to normal operation. Spend points on upgrades to improve your de-bugging skills and efficiency.

Controls

  • Click the bug in the play arena to squash it. The bug jumps to a new location after each squash and scurries in a new direction.
  • Buy Stronger Click, Auto Squash, and Bug Spray when the buttons highlight. Gray buttons cost more than your current score.
  • Auto-squash upgrades keep squashing bugs even when you are not actively clicking but does not teleport the bug like normal clicks. Bug Spray raises both manual and auto click power.
  • Reset run asks for confirmation, then wipes score, upgrades, and the auto timer.

Scoring

Each squash awards points. Upgrades raise click power, add idle squashes, or both.

Progress saves in this browser. Refresh keeps the run. Use Reset run if you want a clean start.

At 50 bugs squashed, the header flips from OFFLINE to ONLINE.
