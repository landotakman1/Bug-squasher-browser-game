How to Play

Objective

Bugs have crashed the production server! Your goal as the new Jr. Developer is to "squash" as many bugs as possible to restore the system to normal operation. Spend points on upgrades to improve your de-bugging skills and efficiency.

Controls

  • Click Squash Bug in the play arena. The target button jumps to a new location after each squash.
  • Buy Stronger Click, Auto Squash, and Bug Spray when the buttons highlight. Gray buttons cost more than your current score.
  • Auto-squash upgrades keep squashing bugs even when you are not actively clicking. Bug Spray raises both manual and auto click power.

Scoring

Each squash awards points. Better upgrades increase your squash power or add auto-squashes over time. Game state will reset on refresh.
